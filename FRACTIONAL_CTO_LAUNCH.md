## Discovery Call Framework (If Founder Says Yes)

### Opening (2 min)
*"Thanks for making time. I work with founders at your stage on technical decisions — usually 6-12 months pre-Series C. Mostly I help you map out what's actually slowing you down vs. what feels urgent but doesn't matter. Does that sound useful?"*

### Three Questions (15 min)
1. **"What are the 3 biggest technical decisions you're facing in the next 6 months?"**
   - Listen for: Infrastructure gaps, scaling, debt, hiring, tool choices
   - Your goal: Understand if you can help

2. **"How much of your engineering roadmap is new features vs. keeping the lights on?"**
   - Listen for: Tech debt ratio (if 50/50 or worse, they need help)
   - Your goal: Quantify the problem

3. **"What does your Series C investor diligence look like?"**
   - Listen for: What they're worried about (security, scaling, architecture, hiring)
   - Your goal: Position your help as investor-prep

### Proposal (5 min)
*"Here's what I typically do: 5-hour technical audit (costs nothing), then we loop into a 10-hour/week retainer for 3-6 months where you throw infrastructure and strategy questions at me. Pace is whatever works for you. Does that feel useful?"*

### Close (1 min)
- If they say yes: "Let's schedule the audit. When works for you in the next week?"
- If they say maybe: "I'll send you a calendar link, pick whatever works."
- If they say no: "No worries — save me for when you're 12 months pre-Series C."
